[Reviewed gallery](README.md) | [Needs-work audit](needs-work.md) | [Full catalogue](../mandel-catalog/README.md)

**needs-work**. The canyon walls, water channel and distant central ridges correspond in the neutral view. Native authored illumination creates a bright golden water surface and luminous atmospheric depth. FPT leaves the water and much of the foreground wall nearly dark, with substantially weaker readable ripples and no comparable distant glow. This materially changes water visibility and scene illumination, beyond a palette difference.
